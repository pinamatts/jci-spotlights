Member Spotlight: Johnel P. Luceño 🎤

Congratulations to our 2027 Executive Vice President for External Affairs, Johnel "Nel" P. Luceño, a 2026 JCI Philippines Presidential Awardee for his service as National Chairperson for Debate and Public Speaking.

Nel knows the stage from both sides. In 2025 he helped the Visayas Area team reclaim the JCI Philippines National Debate Championship, and he was named one of the Top 5 Most Outstanding Members at the National Convention in Legazpi City. In 2026 he carried that title to the JCI Asia-Pacific Conference in Niigata, Japan, where he reached the debate semifinals representing the Philippines, and he served the whole country as the national chair of the program that shaped him.

Off the stage, Nel is the proponent of #MoveEveryday. It began as his own daily habit while training for IRONMAN 70.3, and it grew into a JCI Cebu movement, starting with the #MoveEveryday Community Run for Bins in 2024. He has since crossed the finish line at IRONMAN 70.3 Lapu-Lapu. In his own words: "It starts with showing up."

By day, he is a Software Engineering Manager at Accenture. In JCI Cebu, he has served as Vice President for Community Development and as Auditor, and he now serves as Executive Vice President for External Affairs in our 79th year.

Congratulations, Nel. JCI Cebu is proud of you.

Member Spotlight: Dr. Pedro T. Fernan III, CLSSBB 🏆

Congratulations to our 2027 Executive Treasurer, Dr. Pedro "Pidot" T. Fernan III, CLSSBB, named CEO of the Year for the BPO, Recruitment, and Talent Management Industry at the Legacy Icon Awards 2026.

The Legacy Council Philippines presented the award on October 26, 2026, at the Grand Ballroom of Okada Manila.

Pidot is the Founder and CEO of Level Up Talent Solutions, a Cebu-based company that trains and places skilled virtual assistants and helps growing businesses with outsourcing and recruitment. He is also a Certified Lean Six Sigma Black Belt, and he brings that discipline to how his teams are trained.

In JCI Cebu, he served as Director for Recruitment in 2025 and Vice President for Organizational Development in 2026, and he will serve as Executive Treasurer in our 79th year. Every Christmas, he and his wife also share a meal with street dwellers in Cebu, a quiet tradition that says as much about him as any award.

After the awards night, Pidot wrote that legacy "is not measured by how much we earn… but by the lives we touch," and he shared the recognition with his family, his Level Up team, and the clients and partners who trusted them.

Congratulations, Pidot. JCI Cebu is proud of you.

Member Spotlight: Engr. Jay Emanuel Q. Alegarbes, MSc 🏅

Congratulations to our 2027 Vice President for Linkages, Engr. Jay Emanuel Q. Alegarbes, MSc, recipient of the 2026 Golden Laurel Award for Scientific and/or Technological Development, conferred by JCI Philippines at the 2026 National Convention in Zamboanga.

Jay is an industrial engineer, and an instructor and researcher at the University of San Carlos. His research applies artificial intelligence to a solar-powered hydroponics system, work he presented for the Philippines at the 11th World Sustainability Forum in Barcelona in 2025. That same thinking sits behind Project BERDE, JCI Cebu's food security program, which was named Best Food Security and Sustainability Program at this year's Temiong Awards.

The Golden Laurel adds to a growing record:
🔹 Outstanding Presenter, Advances in Renewable Energy, RACCETCon and SEECON 2026
🔹 Research Presenter, 11th World Sustainability Forum, Barcelona, 2025
🔹 Most Outstanding Danawanon in Education, City of Danao, 2024
🔹 Best in Research Paper, Second Runner-up, 19th PIIE National Congress, 2024

In JCI, Jay has served as Local Secretary General and Vice President for Community Development of JCI Cebu, and as Area Secretary of JCI Visayas Area. He also writes information materials on the rights of women and children for the City of Danao, in Cebuano.

Congratulations, Jay. JCI Cebu is proud of you.

Member Spotlight: Kyle Alexander C. Aznar

Some of the proudest moments in JCI Cebu's story are written by our members, long after the meeting ends.

Kyle Alexander C. Aznar served on the JCI Cebu Board of Directors as Auditor in 2025. Outside the chapter, he is President and CEO of Aznar Shipping Corp., the Cebu-based inter-island shipping company his family built.

Kyle stepped up to lead the family business at a young age. He brought in a professional board of directors, modernized how the company works and kept the fleet growing. The Cebu Chamber of Commerce and Industry has named him its Young Entrepreneur of the Year.

Aznar Shipping's roots go back to 1992. Its first RoRo vessel, Melrivic 2, began sailing the Tuburan–Escalante route in 1993. Today its RoRo and passenger vessels connect communities across the Visayas, and a modernization program has added the Manoling vessels to the fleet.

Now the company is writing its next chapter. Aznar Shipping is listing on the Philippine Stock Exchange this December, allowing investors to take part in Visayas’ shipping growth. The offer period runs from November 26 to December 9, 2026, at PX per share, with the PSE debut on December 18, 2026.

JCI Cebu has been developing young leaders for nearly eight decades. Watching one of our own take his family's company to the stock exchange is a proud moment for the chapter and a page in our own history.

Aznar Shipping is also a Diamond partner of JCI Cebu's 79th year. Thank you, Kyle, for standing with the chapter, and congratulations to you and the whole Aznar Shipping team.

Learn more about the company: https://aznarshipping.ph
